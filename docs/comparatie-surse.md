# Competitor fact base: Burmatex vs Interface vs modulyss vs DESSO (Tarkett) vs Mohawk Group

Research date: 12 Nov 2026. Sources are manufacturer sites, regulatory filings (SEC 10-K, Companies House, Euronext/AMF) and trade press. "Claimed" means the manufacturer's own claim. "Certified" means there is a third-party certificate. **[Judgment]** marks our interpretation. Facts are stated per product or per collection. Do not generalise them to a whole brand unless the source does.

Source: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00596538/persons-with-significant-control

## 2. Interface, Inc.

**Ownership / company**
- Interface, Inc. was incorporated in Georgia, USA, in 1973. HQ is in Atlanta. It is listed on Nasdaq (TILE). Brands: Interface and FLOR (carpet tile), and nora (rubber flooring). FY2025 net sales were US$1,387m. Sources: https://s205.q4cdn.com/354928249/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Interface-Inc-2025-10-K.pdf ; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260224280163/en/
- Principal plants in Europe, Africa, Asia and Australia ("EAAA") per the 2025 10-K properties table: **Scherpenzeel, Netherlands** (1.25m sq ft, owned), **Craigavon, Northern Ireland** (leased), Weinheim, Germany (nora, leased), Taicang, China, and Minto, Australia. US plants are in LaGrange and West Point, Georgia. Source: 10-K above.
- The Interface UK 2024 Modern Slavery statement says UK-supplied products are made mainly at Scherpenzeel, Weinheim and Craigavon. Source: https://s205.q4cdn.com/354928249/files/doc_downloads/esg-resources/2025/11/Interface-UK-Modern-Slavery-2024.pdf

**Positioning / price**
- No trade source compares Interface's price tier with the other four brands. **Not stated.** The 10-K says the Open Air™ collection (2021) was designed "at a lower price point", which suggests Interface sells across more than one price tier. Source: 10-K.

**Range / formats**
- Carpet tile is "typically made as 50 cm x 50 cm tiles as well as planks in varying sizes" (10-K). Skinny Planks are 25 × 100 cm and are designed to combine with 50 × 50 cm. Interface also offers LVT and nora rubber. Sources: 10-K ; https://pdf.archiexpo.com/pdf/interface/skinny-planks/4250-290966.html
- Number of collections: not stated. The 10-K says it offers "200+ carbon negative styles".

**Construction / yarn / backing**
- Example products (GB site):
  - Composure: tufted structured loop, 100% solution-dyed nylon with 75% recycled content. Source: https://shop.interface.com/GB/en-GB/carpet-tile/composure/4169C.html
  - Recreation: 100% recycled solution-dyed nylon (50% pre-consumer, 50% post-consumer). Source: https://shop.interface.com/GB/en-GB/carpet-tile/recreation/4313C.html
- Backings:
  - **CQuest™Bio** is standard on all carpet tiles.
  - **CQuest™BioX** has a higher share of carbon-negative materials and is standard on the carbon-negative products.
  - **CQuest™Bio SONE** gives impact sound reduction up to 29 dB and αw up to 0.30.
  - **CQuest™Bio SONE+** gives up to 33 dB and αw 0.30–0.40.
  - GlasBac® is a platform used mainly in the Americas, per the 10-K.
  - Sources: https://www.interface.com/GB/en-GB/carpet-tile/backings/ ; 10-K
- Interface describes the CQuest backings as "net carbon negative" when measured **on a stand-alone basis**. This applies to the backing materials, not the whole tile. Source: 10-K.

**Sustainability**
- "Cradle-to-gate carbon negative" tiles started in 2020 with the Embodied Beauty™ collection (Shishu Stitch, Tokyo Texture, Zen Stitch on CQuestBioX). Interface now claims "200+ carbon negative styles". This is a cradle-to-gate (A1–A3) claim, not a full life-cycle claim. Sources: 10-K ; https://shop.interface.com/GB/en-GB/carpet-tile/carbon-negative
- Product-level embodied carbon (cradle to gate, from GB product pages): Composure 2.94 kg CO₂e/m², Recreation 2.25 kg CO₂e/m². Both are positive values, so these standard products are **not** carbon negative. EPDs are downloadable (dated 16-10-2025). Sources: product pages above.
- Cradle to Cradle: since Jan 2025, all European-made products with CQuestBio™ backing and Aquafil yarn (75+ styles) are certified **C2C Silver**. Recreation's page shows C2C v3.1 Silver. Source: https://interface.com/GB/en-GB/more-from-interface/press-room/interface-expands-cradle-to-cradle-certified-carpet-tile-options-in-europe
- Corporate targets: science-based targets by 2030 and a goal to become a "carbon negative enterprise by 2040" (10-K). The 2025 10-K does **not** mention the earlier "Carbon Neutral Floors" (offset-based) programme. We could not confirm whether that programme is still offered in Europe.
- **ReEntry®** take-back has run since 1995. The routes are reuse, recycling (ReEntry 2.0 separates yarn and backing) and energy recovery. Recycling capacity at Scherpenzeel was expanded in Sep 2024. Sources: https://www.interface.com/GB/en-GB/more-from-interface/press-room/interface-expands-recycling-capabilities-in-europe ; https://www.interface.com/content/dam/interfaceinc/interface/sustainability/emea/reentry/brochure/EMEA%20ReEntry%20Brochure%20EN.pdf

**Performance**
- Composure and Recreation are both rated EN 1307 class 33 "Heavy Contract", Euroclass Bfl-s1, and ΔLw 23 dB (EN ISO 10140-3) on the standard backing. Each has a **15-year commercial product warranty**. Sources: product pages above.
- 10-K: carpet warranties are limited (surface wear, edge ravel, static) and run **10–20 years** depending on product and environment.

**Installation**
- **TacTiles®** are adhesive connector squares that join tiles to each other to make a "floating floor". There is no liquid adhesive, and Interface says VOCs are "virtually" zero. Product pages say tiles are "optimised for glue-free installation with TacTiles". Sources: https://www.interface.com/content/dam/interfaceinc/interface/technical/emea/tactiles/tactiles-brochure/TacTiles_EU.pdf ; product pages.
- Correction: "Sound Choice" is Interface's **LVT** acoustic backing (Sound Choice+ up to 19 dB). It is not a carpet-tile backing. The carpet-tile acoustic backings are SONE and SONE+. Source: https://www.interface.com/GB/en-GB/carpet-tile/backings/

**Presence in Romania**
- Interface's official locations page lists **no office or showroom in Romania** or anywhere in CEE (PL/CZ/HU). Nearest listed offices: Wels, Austria, and the EMEA HQ in Scherpenzeel. Source: https://shop.interface.com/GB/en-GB/locations/
- Romanian dealer: **Corporate Office Solutions (COS)**, Bucharest. COS became InterfaceFLOR dealer in 2009. AmCham Romania (22 Jun 2023) says COS "has exclusive partnerships in Romania with suppliers such as Steelcase and Interface". Sources: https://www.bursa.ro/corporate-office-solutions-a-devenit-dealer-interfaceflor-in-romania-0583950 ; https://www.amcham.ro/news-from-members/cos-celebrates-25-years-of-pioneering-the-romanian-design--build-market
- Bucharest reference projects with Interface (e.g. Nestlé, about 3,000 m²) appear on officesnapshots.com. Source: https://officesnapshots.com/?p=111876 (COS office). Project pages are illustrative only.
- **Our "Interface Romania office in Bucharest" premise was NOT verified.** The evidence points to an exclusive dealer (COS), not an Interface-owned office.

## 3. modulyss (Belysse Group)

**Ownership / company**
- modulyss was founded in **2010** and is HQ'd in Zele, Belgium (Zevensterrestraat 21). It is "a part of Belysse", alongside itc and Bentley (Bentley Mills, USA). It has showrooms in London, Ghent and Paris. Source: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/about-modulyss
- **Belysse Group NV** (formerly **Balta Group NV**, renamed 2024) is listed on Euronext Brussels (BELYS). In April 2022 Balta sold its rugs, residential PP and non-woven businesses, together with the "Balta" brand, to Victoria PLC. It kept the commercial brands modulyss and Bentley. Source: https://loyensloeff.com/insights/news--events/news/loyens--loeff-congratulates-balta-group-nv-with-the-completion-of-the-sale-of-its-rugs-residential-pp-and-non-woven-businesses-to-victoria
- The controlling shareholder is **Lone Star Funds** via LSF9 Balta Holdco S.à r.l., with about 54% (aggregator figure; check the latest Euronext transparency filing). Sources: https://simplywall.st/stocks/de/consumer-durables/fra-bdb/belysse-group-shares/ownership ; https://www.beursgenoten.nl/koersen/euronext-brussel/balta/profiel
- Manufacturing site: the modulyss site does not state it. Industry knowledge places it at the Belysse/modulyss Belgian plant, but **not verified from a primary source**. **[Caveat]**
- Trade press reported a 12.2% revenue decline at modulyss (Europe) in a recent period, on weak European demand. Source: https://interiordaily.com/article/9819985/belysse-floorcoverings-group-revenue-declines-amid-weak-residential-market/ ; FY2025 results: https://live.euronext.com/sites/default/files/company_press_releases/attachments/2026/03/13/Connect_belysse-fy-2025-resultsprenfinal.pdf

**Positioning / price**
- Not stated by any trade source.

**Range / formats**
- Products: carpet tiles, broadloom and LVT. Source: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT
- Tile formats: **50×50, 100×100, 50×100 and 25×100 cm**. Non-standard sizes are typically optional from 100 m² (e.g. Grind, First Forward). Sources: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/carpet/carpet-tiles ; https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/carpet/collections/first-forward
- The carpet collections page lists **41 collections** (our count of collection links, Nov 2026; some include broadloom). Source: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/carpet/collections

**Construction / yarn / backing**
- Yarns: PA6, **ECONYL®** regenerated nylon in many collections (Grind is 100% ECONYL®), plus solution-dyed, piece-dyed and Chromojet-printed options. Sources: carpet-tiles page ; https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/carpet/collections/grind
- Tile backings (source: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/carpet/carpet-tiles/backings):

  | Backing | Type | Recycled content | Other stated facts |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **ecoBACK PLUS** | polyolefin plus bio-based materials | 85% recycled and renewable | 48% lighter than Back2Back |
  | **ecoBACK** | PVC- and bitumen-free | 75% recycled | — |
  | **comfortBACK ECO** | felt | 60% recycled | average 65% better sound absorption (no dB given) |
  | **comfortBACK** | felt plus bitumen | 90% recycled PES | — |
  | **Back2Back®** | bitumen | 75% recycled | — |

- **No dB/αw values** are published on the backings page.
- Correction: "Pure modulyss backing" was not found on the current site.

**Sustainability**
- "Limit Your Footprint" programme offering "a circular and CO2 neutral floor". Source: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/designing-for-a-better-future
- The CO2-neutral mechanism (**CO2RE**, developed with CO2logic) **uses carbon credits / offsets**: credits are purchased for the remaining emissions (e.g. Paradigm Project, Kenya). It is standard on the Handcraft collection (Moss, Leaf, Willow, Grind) and available on request for other collections. The claim is **offset-based, not a reduction to zero**. Source: https://eboss.co.nz/ebossnow/heritage-carpets-modulyss-handcraft-collection-reduce-carbon-emissions **[Judgment / legal caution]** Under the EU Empowering Consumers Directive (2024/825), offset-based "climate neutral" claims face restrictions from 2026. Wording should be cautious.
- Cradle to Cradle: a "Cradle to Cradle Certified Silver" badge appears on the sustainability page. Grind is C2C **Full Scope V4.0 Silver**. Sources: sustainability page ; Grind page.
- EPDs: product-specific EPDs per backing (EN 15804, IBU programme). Example A1–A3 GWP is about 12.2–18.2 kg CO₂e/m², depending on product weight. Source: https://materials.oneclicklca.com/en/material/tufted-carpet-tiles/621ba70920d37f41c9e0ace9
- "Take Back. Give Back." end-of-life programme: reuse first, then recycling. Science-based targets for 2030: −42% Scope 1+2 versus 2023 and −25% Scope 3. Up to 70% recycled content in several collections. Source: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/designing-for-a-better-future

**Performance**
- Class 33 is stated on the carpet tiles page. A **15-year guarantee** applies to carpet tiles. Fire class is not stated on the pages we read; check each collection's datasheet. Sources: carpet-tiles page ; about page.

**Presence in Romania**
- modulyss's contact page lists only Zele HQ. The country selector has no Romania option, and there is no dealer locator. Source: https://www.modulyss.com/en-INT/contact
- **No Romanian modulyss dealer was found** in public sources. Unverified lead: Vaalbara, a Bucharest flooring distributor, but it does not name modulyss. Source: https://issuu.com/vaalbara.ro/docs/vaalbara_showroom

## 4. DESSO (Tarkett)

**Ownership / company**
- DESSO was founded in **1930 in Oss, NL**, as a maker of woven carpets. Source: https://professionals.tarkett.co.uk/en_GB/node/desso-carpet-16235
- Acquired by **Tarkett** from Bencis: announced Oct 2014, completed **2 Jan 2015** (about €160m). Source: https://www.tarkett-group.com/app/uploads/2021/12/23112937/2-01-2015-tarkett-to-complete-desso-acquisition.pdf
- Tarkett SA (Paris) has been **taken private**. Its controlling shareholder, Tarkett Participation, ran a buy-out offer plus squeeze-out at €17/share, and delisting from Euronext Paris was scheduled for **23 Dec 2025**. Sources: https://live.euronext.com/en/products/equities/company-news/2025-12-12-press-release-dated-12-december-2025-regarding ; https://live.euronext.com/en/products/equities/company-news/2025-06-06-autorite-marches-financiers-approves-public-buyout-offer
- DESSO plants: **Waalwijk (NL)** and **Dendermonde (BE)**. Source: https://professionals.tarkett.com/en_EU/node/desso-carpet-tile-and-roll-solutions-innovation-functionality-and-sustainability-5527

**Positioning / price**
- Tarkett describes DESSO as having "more than 85 years of experience making high-end carpets". This is a self-description. No trade-source price tier was found. Source: same Tarkett page.

**Range / formats**
- Tarkett Romania lists **71 DESSO collections and 300+ DESSO products** under "Mochetă modulară". Fibre types: 100% PA, PA/PES, 80% wool/20% PA, BCF PA6 and ECONYL. Source: https://www.tarkett.ro/ro_RO/categorie-rs_C01018-mocheta-modulara
- Formats include 50×50 squares (e.g. AirMaster Reflection) and 25×100 plank options (e.g. Fuse Create). DESSO also offers broadloom ("rolls") and rugs. Sources: https://source.thenbs.com/en/gb/product/desso-airmaster-reflection/f6JUvXX2ATjyJnFez6nuVS/bjC8ABebzuWo9Rzu92MT1Z ; https://source.thenbs.com/product/fuse-create-ecobasetm/dMjnvffY4z9WZJKcQ8JkxH

**Construction / backing**
- **EcoBase®**: 80% "positively defined" chalk from 100% recycled material (a by-product of Dutch drinking water companies). Lloyd's Register assures it is 100% recyclable. **C2C Gold** (Platinum for material health), certificate no. 6956, v3.1. Recycling EcoBase tiles is said to save up to 20% CO₂ per product cycle versus cement co-processing. Sources: https://professionals.tarkett.co.uk/en_GB/node/desso-ecobase-8048 ; https://c2ccertified.org/certified-products/desso-ecobase-pa6-solution-dyed-carpet-tiles-gold
- 62% of Tarkett carpet tiles have EcoBase as standard. Source: https://professionals.tarkett.co.uk/en_GB/node/going-for-gold-inside-tarkett-s-cradle-to-cradle-carpet-tile-journey-14246
- **SoundMaster®** (acoustic felt, 100% PES, 80% recycled PET):
  - impact sound **+8 dB** over the standard backing;
  - sound absorption up to **+0.15 αw** (up to +100% versus standard carpet);
  - tested by SWA Aachen.
  - Variants: SoundMaster Thrive (100% recyclable) and SoundMaster Lite.
  - Source: https://professionals.tarkett.com/en_EU/node/desso-soundmaster-backing-5204
- Correction: "ProBase" was not found on current Tarkett/DESSO pages.

**Sustainability**
- First carpet-tile maker with **C2C Gold** (2015). The AirMaster collection on EcoBase is Gold. Source: https://prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/desso-receives-cradle-to-cradle-gold-certification-529027351.html
- **ReStart®** take-back covers 29 countries, with 129,000 t collected over 16 years across 8 recycling centres (Tarkett-wide, all flooring types). DESSO tiles go to the Waalwijk recycling centre, where yarn is separated from EcoBase. Sources: https://professionals.tarkett.co.uk/en_GB/node/restart-10623 ; https://www.fcnews.net/2026/04/tarkett-releases-2025-sustainability-statement/
- EPDs are available (e.g. "DESSO carpet tiles, 100% recycled PA6 (max 700 g/m²) + EcoBase"). Source: https://consolidado.tarkett.com.br/images/arquivos/EPD%20-%20PA6_Ecobase_700g_17333290445383.pdf
- No "carbon neutral" or "carbon negative" tile claim was found for DESSO.

**Performance**
- Typical spec: EN 1307 **class 33**, **Bfl-s1** (often "loose laid tested"). NBS listings show a **10-year** warranty for AirMaster Reflection and Sphere. Sources: NBS links above ; https://source.thenbs.com/product/desso-desert/jt1U25kzSsijZ4Jm6zHUgS

**Installation**
- Glue-free / tackifier systems: not verified on the pages read. Check the installation guide.

**Presence in Romania**
- **Tarkett showroom in Bucharest**: Willbrook Platinum Business & Convention Centre, Șos. București–Ploiești 172–176, Building A, 3rd floor, Sector 1. Tel. +40 21 224 0124. Visits by appointment. Romanian-language site tarkett.ro with a distributor finder. Sources: https://www.tarkett.ro/ro_RO/node/contactati-ne-4892 ; https://www.tarkett.ro/ro_RO/magazin

## 5. Mohawk Group (EMEA) / Mohawk Industries

**Ownership / company**
- **Mohawk Industries, Inc.** is HQ'd in Calhoun, Georgia, and listed on NYSE (MHK). It traces back to 1878 (Amsterdam, NY). It acquired Unilin (Belgium) in 2005 and IVC Group in 2015. Sources: https://mohawkind.com/about ; https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Mohawk_Industries (secondary)
- **Mohawk Group EMEA** is the former **IVC Commercial** carpet-tile business, renamed 28 Mar 2024. It is "a brand of Unilin", described as "the premium commercial flooring brand of Unilin within the EMEA market", and Unilin is part of Mohawk Industries. Sources: https://www.mohawkgroup.eu/en/about ; https://www.floordaily.net/flooring-news/ivc-commercial-rebranded-as-mohawk-group-emea
- **Made in Belgium**: production is in **Moeskroen/Mouscron**, with a showroom at the Flooring Development Centre in Waregem. The plant was announced by IVC/Mohawk in Jan 2017 to start production by end of 2017. Sources: floordaily rebrand article ; https://floordaily.net/?p=24273
- The US "Mohawk Group" (mohawkgroup.com) is a separate regional affiliate with its own product lines (e.g. EcoFlex Matrix). Do not mix US specs into EU comparisons. Source: https://www.mohawkgroup.com/press-releases/latest-press-releases/ecoflex-matrix-ulc-2025

**Positioning / price**
- Self-described as "premium". No independent trade price-tier source was found.

**Range / formats**
- Ranges: Signature concepts, Elements and Our Essentials. Collections include Imperfection, Rudiments, Pattern, Balanced, Tailored, Unchained, Light Art and Art Intervention, among others. The site lists **269 floors** (SKUs). Sources: https://www.mohawkgroup.eu/en ; https://www.mohawkgroup.eu/en/carpet-tiles
- Formats in the product filter: **500 × 500 mm and 250 × 1000 mm**. Source: https://www.mohawkgroup.eu/en/carpet-tiles (filter data)

**Construction / yarn / backing**
- Yarn filters: 100% solution-dyed nylon, ECONYL®, and "100% SDN Thrive® Matter".
  - Thrive Matter is described on the site as "The world's first carbon negative nylon yarn, contains 90% recycled content".
  - Thrive is a **Universal Fibers** yarn, not a Mohawk product. Its carbon-negative status is that supplier's claim.
  - Sources: https://www.mohawkgroup.eu/en/carpet-tiles ; https://www.floortrendsmag.com/articles/108032-universal-fibers-introduces-new-carbon-negative-fiber
- Backings (EU):
  - **EcoFlex™ NXT**: polyolefin.
  - **EcoFlex™ One**: NXT plus an acoustic comfort layer, with recycled materials.
  - **EcoFlex™ Echo**: Statera plus an acoustic felt.
  - **EcoFlex™ Statera**: modified bitumen.
  - **No dB, αw or recycled-content figures are published on the EU backings page.**
  - Source: https://www.mohawkgroup.eu/en/backings

**Sustainability**
- The EU site publishes a sustainability data sheet in its document library. No EU-specific carbon-neutral, C2C or take-back programme was found on the pages read. **Not verified.**

**Performance / installation**
- Class, fire rating, warranty and glue-free installation are **not verified** for the EU range. These are in the document library (/en/documents).

**Presence in Romania**
- The Mohawk Group EMEA country selector covers EN, UK, BE, NL, FR, DE and PL, not Romania. There is no Romanian office. Contact: Industriëlaan 97a, 7700 Moeskroen, info@mohawkgroup.eu. Source: https://www.mohawkgroup.eu/en
- An IVC-era carpet-tile brochure ("Balanced Hues") is hosted on perpetuum.ro, which suggests past or present resale via Perpetuum. **Not confirmed.** Source: https://www.perpetuum.ro/pdfs/CT_Balanced%20Hues_IVC_BROCHURE_19x29,7cm_INTERNATIONAAL_SC079AFC033Z_4PP_20874.pdf

## Pros / cons per brand (for a Romanian commercial buyer)

### Burmatex
**Pros**
1. Independent, UK-made specialist. Its parent AIREA plc is a listed company with public annual reporting, which supports supplier due diligence. Sources: Companies House PSC ; https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/airea--aiea/final-results-for-the-year-ended-31-december-2025/9492481
2. Proprietary BioBase® "low carbon backing system" made from recycled materials. Source: https://www.burmatex.co.uk/
3. Official Romanian dealer (InsightFLOOR) with go to® stock held in Romania (user-supplied). This is a lead-time advantage versus brands with no local stock. **[Judgment]**
4. Take Back Service for end-of-life tiles. Source: https://www.burmatex.co.uk/sustainability-principles/

**Cons**
1. Much smaller group: AIREA revenue is about £21m versus Interface's US$1.39bn. A smaller group usually has a smaller local specification and support team. **[Judgment]** Sources: AIREA RNS ; Interface 10-K
2. No published C2C certification found on the pages read (SKA Silver only). Source: https://www.burmatex.co.uk/sustainability-principles/
3. Made outside the EU (UK), so customs and documentation apply for Romanian imports since 2021. **[Judgment, general trade fact]**

### Interface
**Pros**
1. Largest public carpet-tile specialist of the five (US$1.39bn sales in 2025), with European plants in NL and Northern Ireland. Source: 10-K
2. Cradle-to-gate carbon-negative styles (200+). Embodied carbon is published per product, and EPDs are downloadable. Sources: 10-K ; product pages
3. Glue-free TacTiles® system and acoustic SONE/SONE+ backings (up to 33 dB ΔLw, αw up to 0.40). Source: https://www.interface.com/GB/en-GB/carpet-tile/backings/
4. 15-year warranty, class 33, Bfl-s1 on example products. C2C Silver on European CQuestBio/Aquafil styles. Sources: product pages ; C2C press release
5. Exclusive Romanian partner (COS) with a long track record since 2009. Source: AmCham 2023

**Cons**
1. No Interface-owned office or showroom in Romania or CEE; the nearest is Wels, AT. Source: locations page

- **modulyss Romanian dealer:** none found. modulyss manufacturing site: not confirmed from a primary source. "Pure modulyss backing": not found on the current site.
